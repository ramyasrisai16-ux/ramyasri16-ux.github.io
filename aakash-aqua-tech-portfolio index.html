FAQ

Everything you need to know

Common questions about our RO water purifiers, maintenance, installation and after-sales service.

An RO (Reverse Osmosis) water purifier removes dissolved salts, heavy metals, chemicals, bacteria, viruses and other harmful impurities — giving you clean, safe and great-tasting drinking water.
If your water TDS is above 500 ppm, or you use borewell, tanker or hard water, an RO purifier is recommended. We provide a free water quality check to help you choose the right one.
TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) measures the concentration of dissolved minerals, salts and impurities present in water.
  • Sediment Filter — every 3–6 months
  • Carbon Filter — every 6–12 months
  • RO Membrane — every 12–24 months
Modern RO systems with Mineralizer, Alkaline Filter or TDS Controller restore essential minerals like calcium and magnesium for healthier drinking water.
